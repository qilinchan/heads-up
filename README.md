# Heads Up!

A phone guessing game for family and friends. One player holds the phone on
their forehead and the others give clues.

- **Tilt down** = correct, **tilt up** = pass (or tap right/left side).
- Rounds are 90 seconds (tap the timer button to switch to 60 or 120).
- Categories: Animals, Fruits, Food, Asian, School, Music, Cello & Violin,
  Sports, Movies & Characters, Jobs, Around the House, Act It Out, Places,
  Nature & Weather, Vehicles, Sound It Out, Minnesota, China, Sing It Out,
  Play It Out: Suzuki Cello 1 & 2, Play It Out: Suzuki Violin 1 & 2,
  and Mix It Up (all of them).
- Scores are saved on the phone for each player (tap "+" to add players).
  Nothing is sent anywhere: no accounts, tracking or network requests.

Edit `words.js` to change or add word lists.
